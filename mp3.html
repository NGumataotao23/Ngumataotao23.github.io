Linked List mp3 player
Final for my Data Structures class, it uses a Linked list to hold mp3 files in order to play
them in order like an mp3 player. It uses the SDL2 library to play the actual mp3s in the terminal.
Songs can be queued,removed, and you can queue a song right before the next one. It uses a thread to
facilitate the beginning of the song and you can do any of the controls while the song is playing.
