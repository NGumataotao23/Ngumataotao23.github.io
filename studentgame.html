Student Life sim Game
This is the culimination of a semesters worth of assignemnts in my object oriented class, all of it is written in Java.
It is a life simulation game of a Student, the Student is represented by a solid red traingle while
other students are marked with unfilled red triangles. Blue building are meant to be lecture halls
which any student can enter and cannot leave until the lecture is over. The other objects are bathrooms
and water fountains. The panel on the right gives vital info to the player such as their hydration, if their hyrdation reaches
0 the game is over. The software is writte for the CodeNameOne Interface which is an interaface specifically for mobile development.
